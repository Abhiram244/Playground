# Safety and table contract

Status: setup; approved portions only.

## Tone and themes

- Tone: logical, with a mix of tonal modes.
- Main play interests: preparation, combat and competition, roleplay, and
  romance.
- Other themes may be mixed in only as they remain consistent with the safety
  boundaries and later player approvals.

## Boundaries

- Line: no body horror. It must not be depicted or introduced as active content.
- Veil: torture and other sensitive topics fade to black. Do not describe their
  details.
- No unstated safety consent should be inferred.
- If proposed content may approach an unspecified boundary, pause for player
  approval before introducing it.

## Creative authority during setup

The listed inspirations and academic university-like frame are ingredients for
collaborative design. They do not authorize the GM to independently create the
setting, plot, factions, cosmology, character, or opening situation.
